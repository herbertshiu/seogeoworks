## Cover
Signal Room
SEO & GEO, in the open
Feature and architecture overview · September 2026

## Slide 1
### A field guide for the discoverability era
Signal Room turns SEO/GEO education into an editorial product designed to earn attention, trust, and backlinks.

- Audience: marketers, founders, content teams, and search practitioners
- Positioning: independent voices, practical frameworks, and tools for modern search
- Growth role: publish useful, citeable expertise that compounds organic visibility

## Slide 2
### One publication, four high-intent experiences
The homepage is structured as a guided journey from curiosity to action.

- Editorial briefings make the topic legible and worth returning to
- SEO vs GEO comparison turns strategy differences into a visual matrix
- Case study and dispatch sections make the ideas concrete and shareable
- Readiness, ROI, and audit tools convert attention into self-discovery

## Slide 3
### The comparison matrix makes the strategy gap visible
Visitors can toggle between All gaps, SEO lens, and GEO lens while scanning six practical dimensions.

- Outcome: earn the click vs. earn inclusion in an answer
- Authority: links and relevance vs. experts, corroboration, and entities
- Measurement: rankings and conversions vs. citations, sentiment, and qualified demand
- Every row ends with a “Watch for” explanation and a readiness CTA

## Slide 4
### Interactive tools move visitors from insight to action
The site is designed to let visitors test their own situation, not just read about someone else’s.

- ROI calculator: sessions, conversion rate, customer value, GEO lift, and investment
- Readiness self-assessment: six guided questions with separate SEO and GEO scores
- GEO audit generator: nine checks, grouped progress, priorities, and report state
- Browser-only state keeps the experience fast, private, and friction-light

## Slide 5
### The audit generator turns ambiguity into an action queue
The GEO audit organizes the work into three operating systems.

- Identity & expertise: named entities, authors, point of view
- Source quality: evidence, definitions, freshness, and citable context
- Reach & measurement: third-party corroboration, prompt testing, and business outcomes
- The generated report surfaces the first unfinished item as the recommended next focus

## Slide 6
### A deliberately lightweight frontend architecture
The experience is implemented as a static React application with a small, reusable state model.

- React + TypeScript + Vite provide a fast client-only runtime
- Wouter-style section navigation is represented through smooth anchor scrolling
- Local component state powers calculators, toggles, checklists, reports, and subscription feedback
- CSS custom properties, responsive media queries, and Lucide icons keep the system cohesive
- No backend is required for the current interactive layer; build output is deployment-ready

## Slide 7
### A visual system built to feel like a signal room
The design uses contrast, rhythm, and editorial texture to make technical strategy feel human.

- Dark editorial base with electric lime, coral, blue, and violet signal accents
- Playfair Display for point of view; DM Sans for utility; DM Mono for metadata
- CSS-built orbital signal map and structured cards avoid heavy media dependencies
- Light mode preserves the hierarchy while making long-form exploration more comfortable

## Slide 8
### The next layer is a compounding content engine
The site is ready to grow from a feature-rich homepage into a durable SEO/GEO publication.

- Add dedicated article and expert profile routes with structured metadata
- Connect report capture and newsletter signup when a backend is introduced
- Expand case studies with verified first-party outcome data
- Use recurring dispatches to build topical authority and referral pathways
